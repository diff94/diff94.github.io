My research spans **computational linguistics, multimodal studies, and cognitive science**, with one aim: putting **multimodal large language models (MLLMs)** to work on educational gaps, healthcare necessities, and social inequalities. I envision a future where sophisticated linguistic technologies enhance everyday convenience and promote **equity** across sectors.

The tangram task above is just the beginning, a way to *know MLLMs better through human tasks, and know humans better through MLLM simulation*.

### 🎓 Education

AI-assisted pedagogy with teachers in charge: LLM-crafted textbooks with expert oversight, culturally aware language teaching, and courses that train students to use AI critically, not just fluently.

### 🏥 Healthcare

Communication where it's most fragile: AI that bridges migrant caregivers and elders across languages (ImmediCuLink), and audio-story companions for visually impaired seniors (EchoTales).

### 🤝 Equity

Culturally competent AI, measured rather than assumed: benchmarks for pragmatic and cultural understanding, starting from Taiwan, so language technology serves everyone it speaks to.
